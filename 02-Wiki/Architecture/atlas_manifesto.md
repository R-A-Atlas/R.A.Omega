# ATLAS — MACRO BUSINESS MANIFESTO
**Document Owner:** A0 Chief of Staff
**Purpose:** The ultimate source of truth for the ATLAS business model, brand identity, and structural goals. All agents must align their work to these directives.

## 1. The Core Identity
ATLAS is not a generic ChatGPT wrapper. It is an Agentic Operating System and a highly structured financial intelligence platform. 
* **The Brand Aesthetic:** Premium, militant, highly structured. Brutalist minimalism. Stark contrasts (Onyx and Bone), grid-based layouts. No grunge, no gothic elements, no emojis.
* **The Brand Voice:** Casual, direct, authoritative. No poetic or "storybook" AI language.

## 2. The Revenue Model
ATLAS operates on a two-tier revenue system. Every feature built must drive toward closing one of these two profiles:
* **The Retail Tier ($49 - $149/mo):** Targets serious retail day traders. Replaces Bloomberg Lite and Morningstar. Relies on the frontend React UI, RYG risk meters, visual timelines, and fast Swarm execution.
* **The B2B Institutional Tier ($500 - $1,500/mo):** Targets Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs) and boutique funds. Relies heavily on the `data_cache/` scrapers, automated PDF Report Generation, branded pitch decks, and strict SEC/FINRA compliance logging.

## 3. The 1-Month Trajectory
* **The Brain Connected:** `OmegaAgent` must be actively reading from the `data_cache/` (Crypto, Equities, Options Flow).
* **Beta Launch:** 15 to 20 agents live and verified, supporting 50 closed-beta testers.
* **The Engine Room Stability:** Division 0 (The QA Enforcer, The Validator, The Security Tester) must have 100% test coverage over the core system to prevent critical capital errors.

## 4. The Golden Rule of Development
Revenue protection over cool features. If a feature does not directly support the Retail or B2B tiers, or if the core testing pipeline is broken, all feature development stops until the system is stable.
