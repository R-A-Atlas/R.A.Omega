What ATLAS Does That No Other Tool Does

Anti-hallucination architecture
Other AIs guess when data is missing. ATLAS uses null and flags the gap with a SINGLE SOURCE or CONFLICT label. Facts are CONFIRMED before the AI sees them. That's the difference between a report you can trade from and one you can't.
The whisper EPS edge
Knowing the whisper number vs Wall St consensus tells you what traders actually expect. A "beat" where actual EPS exceeds consensus but misses the whisper = sell-off. ChatGPT cannot access this data at all.
Self-improving track record
Most AI tools have no memory of past recommendations. ATLAS has a SQLite database. Every outcome you grade updates the pattern library. Over time, ATLAS will know your personal win rates by setup type better than you do.
Market regime awareness
ATLAS detects whether the market is in Bull, Bear, Chop, or Panic mode and adjusts all confidence scores by a multiplier (0.5x in panic, 1.15x in bull). No general-purpose AI tool does this for trading.
Optional code proposals (CLI)
self_coder.py can draft small patches from graded outcomes — human review only. This is not exposed as a “self-learning” dashboard feature today.
