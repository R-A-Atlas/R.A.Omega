# Brand Guidelines

## Name
**R.A. Omega** — Research & Analysis Omega

## Sub-brand
**ATLAS** — the intelligence layer / agentic OS within R.A. Omega

## Voice
- Institutional but accessible
- Precise, direct, no fluff
- Confident without arrogance
- Data-backed, not opinion-driven

## Tone by Context
- Finance analysis: precise, structured, citing sources
- Chat/casual: warm, direct, conversational
- Error/warning: clear, non-alarming, actionable
- Trade plans (only when requested): professional, exact, no hedging

## Key Phrases (Brand Voice)
- "BOTTOM LINE" — used instead of "Summary" or "TL;DR"
- "THE SETUP" — used for trade setup context
- "HOW THIS PLAYS OUT" — used for scenarios
- "WHAT BREAKS THIS" — used for failure modes / risks
- "YOUR RULES" — used for execution rules
- "INTELLIGENCE BRIEF" — used for trader memo / analyst note
- Input placeholder: "What do you want to know about your money?"

## Visual Identity
- Dark theme (primary)
- Color: teal/cyan accent (#00D4AA or similar)
- Font: Inter
- ATLAS branding in reports
- "117 agents active" footer in standalone reports
- Teal left-border for intelligence memos

## What to Avoid
- Trade jargon on non-trade responses
- Generic financial disclaimer language unless legally required
- Robotic, overly formal language
- Hedging with "I think" or "maybe" in factual finance answers
